## TRANSCRIPT

[JIM MCKENNEY]
Welcome back to The CRA Briefing. I'm Jim Mckenney with a critical infrastructure update on the Cyber Resilience Act.

The European Union Agency for Cybersecurity—ENISA—has finalized the technical interface architecture and API specifications for the Article 16 Single Reporting Platform (SRP).

Beginning September 11, 2026—a full fifteen months before CE marking becomes mandatory—all manufacturers of products with digital elements must submit 24-hour early-warning notifications for any actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe security incidents through this centralized portal.

The SRP implements automated cryptographic dispatching, securely routing early-warning alerts simultaneously to the designated national CSIRTs across all 27 EU Member States.

ENISA has confirmed that machine-readable schema integrations—supporting CSAF 2.0 and JSON-LD payloads—will be available for automated PSIRT operations. This allows engineering teams to connect their vulnerability triage pipelines directly to the European reporting gateway.

If your organization has not yet built automated PSIRT response playbooks for the 24-hour statutory clock, now is the time to start.

Until next time: build secure by design, ship with confidence. I'm Jim Mckenney—thanks for listening.
