[JIM MCKENNEY]
Welcome back to The CRA Briefing. I'm Jim Mckenney with an essential standardisation update on the European Cyber Resilience Act. Standard disclaimer: this briefing provides technical commentary, not formal legal advice.

Here is the top development for hardware and software product engineers: European standardisation bodies CEN, CENELEC, and ETSI have issued an official progress report on Standardisation Request M/606.

The joint technical committees are drafting 41 dedicated European harmonised standards—composed of 15 horizontal standards covering overarching secure lifecycle principles, and 26 vertical standards targeting high-risk product categories such as industrial automation and control systems (IACS), edge computing skids, and smart substation controllers.

Once these standards are approved and formally cited in the Official Journal of the European Union, manufacturers who implement them obtain a legal "presumption of conformity" with the essential cybersecurity requirements set out in Annex I of the CRA.

Crucially for OT equipment vendors, the standardisation working groups are actively harmonizing requirements with the IEC 62443 series and ISO/IEC 27402. This ensures that existing industrial OT cybersecurity investments translate directly into CRA compliance without duplicative certification overhead.

Until next time: build secure by design, ship with confidence. I'm Jim Mckenney—thanks for listening.
