## 1. Statutory & Standardization Framework
* **Standardization Mandate M/606:** Adopted by the European Commission via Implementing Decision C(2025)618, calling for **41 harmonized standards**. Formally accepted by CEN, CENELEC, and ETSI on **3 April 2025**.
* **Three Standardization Tiers:**
  1. **Type A (Horizontal Framework):** CEN/CLC/JTC 13 WG 9 developing the **EN 40000 series** (cyber resilience principles, vocabulary, risk assessment).
  2. **Type B (Product-Agnostic Technical & Vulnerability Handling):** JTC 13 WG 9 developing horizontal vulnerability handling and security measures aligned with CRA Annex I Part II.
  3. **Type C (Vertical Product-Specific):** ETSI TC CYBER (EUSR group) developing the **ETSI EN 304 6xx series** (routers, OS, smart devices, browsers).
* **Deadlines:** Drafting deadlines for Type A/B have been adjusted to **31 October 2026**, Type C to **31 December 2026**, with secondary Type B technical standards stretching into **late 2027**.
* **Status as of August 2026:** **Zero harmonized standards have been published in the OJEU yet**. First OJEU citations are projected for the first half of 2027.

---

## 2. IEC 62443 Harmonization Mechanics
* **IEC 62443-4-2 Amendment:** A dedicated draft amendment to IEC 62443-4-2:2019 is currently being developed internationally to align IACS component cybersecurity directly with CRA Annex I Essential Requirements.
* **IEC 62443-4-1 (Secure Product Development Lifecycle):** Forms the foundational process reference for CRA Annex I Part II vulnerability handling and secure lifecycle management.
* **IEC 62443-3-3 & 2-4:** Serve as system security and integration benchmarks for industrial integrators (EPCs) building multi-component automation skids.
