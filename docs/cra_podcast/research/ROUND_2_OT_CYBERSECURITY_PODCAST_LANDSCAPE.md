# Research Round 2: Global & European ICS/OT Podcast Landscape & CRA Positioning

> **Date:** 2026-08-14  
> **Source:** Perplexity Sonar Deep Market Research  
> **Topic:** Analysis of Established OT Podcasts (Dale Peterson, Waterfall, PrOTect OT, CS2AI, CyberWire Control Loop) vs. EU CRA Coverage

---

## 1. Executive Findings: The Operational vs. Product Compliance Divide
* **The Dominant Operational IT/OT Consensus:** The world's top ICS/OT podcasts (*Unsolicited Response*, *PrOTect OT*, *CS2AI*, *Control Loop*) focus 90%+ of their programming on **operational plant defense**: network packet monitoring, anomaly detection, IT/OT convergence, incident response, and active nation-state cyber warfare (e.g. Sandworm, Volt Typhoon).
* **The Sporadic CRA Footprint:** Only Waterfall's *Industrial Security Podcast* (Andrew Ginter & Nate Nelson with Christina Kiefer) and niche German shows (Rhebo / *OT Security Made Simple* covering Bosch Rexroth's ctrlX, Phoenix Contact's *Mission OT Security*) have dedicated episodes to CRA.
* **The Missing Engineering/CE Dimension:** Zero existing OT podcasts provide a structured, recurring curriculum on **statutory CE marking, Article 21 substantial modifications, CycloneDX firmware SBOM generation, and third-party Notified Body lab preparation**.
