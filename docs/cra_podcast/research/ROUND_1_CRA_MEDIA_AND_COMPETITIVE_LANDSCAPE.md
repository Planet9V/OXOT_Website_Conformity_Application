# Research Round 1: European CRA Media Landscape & Competitive Intelligence

> **Date:** 2026-08-14  
> **Source:** Perplexity Sonar Deep Market Research  
> **Topic:** Existing Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) Podcasts, Media, Law/Consulting Thought Leadership & Competitive Gaps  

---

## 1. Existing Media & Audio Footprint
* **Dedicated Shows:** Only one minor dedicated podcast exists in Europe: *"Ode to Resilience"* (co-hosted by a tech lawyer and engineer). Most other CRA audio content consists of fragmented, isolated episodes within general IT security or EU policy podcasts (e.g. Center for Cybersecurity Policy & Law with MEP Bart Groothuis, RunSafe Security, PrivacyRules PrivacyEspresso, Embedded.com, SafeBreach).
* **Testing & Certification Bodies:** TÜV SÜD touched upon CRA in its *"Passion for Technology"* podcast regarding energy device security. Embedded chip vendors like Digi International and NXP hold compliance webinars. DEKRA, Secura (Bureau Veritas), and BSI have written whitepapers but zero comprehensive podcast series.
* **Law Firms & Big 4:** DLA Piper, Bird & Bird, Covington, PwC, EY, Deloitte produce static PDF client alerts, legal webinars, and generic regulatory overviews. Zero multi-series audio channels dedicated to operational engineering reality.

---

## 2. Critical Market Gaps Identified
1. **Zero Industrial OT & Plant-Floor Depth:** 95% of current media treats CRA as a generic "connected consumer IoT / software" regulation. They ignore SCADA, PLCs, RTUs, DCS, Safety Instrumented Systems (SIS), and Purdue Level 1/2 real-time deterministic constraints.
2. **Missing System Integrator & EPC Focus:** No content addresses how EPC contractors and system integrators (Axians, Spie, Actemium, Bilfinger) navigate Article 21 Substantial Modification risks when combining multi-vendor components into industrial skids.
3. **Absence of Low-Level Embedded Engineering:** Missing concrete engineering discussions on hardware root-of-trust, dual-bank flash secure boot, CycloneDX SBOM automation for RTOS, and crypto-agility.
4. **Lack of Operational Change Management:** Zero practical discussion on coordinating mandatory vulnerability patching with plant maintenance turnarounds, GxP/ATEX re-validation, and avoiding unplanned factory downtime.
