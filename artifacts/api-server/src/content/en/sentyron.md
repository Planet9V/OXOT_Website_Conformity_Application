The Cyber Resilience Act decides which of your products can still be sold in Europe. **Sentyron** is the OXOT × Sentyron answer to it: end-to-end conformity for products with digital elements — IEC 62443 advisory, certified testing, and Notified-Body-grade evidence — delivered in one engagement, from gap assessment to CE mark.

It combines OXOT's IEC 62443-native CRA advisory with Sentyron's OT/ICS testing heritage and Notified-Body pathway, so you meet the Regulation with one partner instead of stitching together an advisor, a test lab and a certifier who each say "it depends."

```keyfacts
Regulation :: [Cyber Resilience Act](/en/cra) — Reg (EU) 2024/2847
Scope :: any product with digital elements placed on the EU market
Per product :: ~6–8 weeks, gap assessment → CE mark
Method :: advisory → testing → certification, one engagement
Standard :: [IEC 62443](/en/iec-62443)-native — SL-T per component, not generic checklists
24-hour clock :: from 11 September 2026 — vulnerability reporting to ENISA + CSIRT
Market-access wall :: 11 December 2027 — no CE mark, no sale
Max penalty :: €15M or 2.5% of global turnover
```

Two firms, one pathway. OXOT sets the conformity strategy and evidence; Sentyron proves it with government-grade testing and — as it is designated — a Notified-Body pathway.
