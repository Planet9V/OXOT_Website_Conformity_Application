# can you please do some deep research of the products that flow into the EU, or are built in the EU that are current? In the context of what is relevatn to CRA? Here's the full research report on CRA-relevant product flows into and within the EU, with deep vendor-level analysis.

The report covers the key dimensions you asked about — here are the headline findings worth flagging for your consulting work:

**Scale is enormous but concentrated.** The EU imported €478B in high-tech products in 2024, with electronics/telecom at 39% of that — but the NB obligation bites only the ~5–10% of products in Class I/II/Critical. In raw revenue terms, that "small" slice covers virtually the entire enterprise IT and OT security stack.[^1]

**The NB bottleneck is the dominant operational risk.** Zero designated CRA NBs exist in NANDO as of today, accreditation takes 12–18 months, and every firewall, hypervisor, and IACS product for NIS2-essential entities needs mandatory NB assessment — with December 2027 just 17 months away. The RED cybersecurity experience already showed what happens when demand exceeds NB capacity — long lead times and delayed CE marking.[^2][^3]

**For your OT client work specifically:** PLCs and DCS are **not** Hardware Security Boxes (Critical/Annex IV)  — most fall into Class I or Class II depending on whether the end-operator is a NIS2 Annex I essential entity. A Siemens S7-1500 in an energy utility = Class II, mandatory NB. The same device in a food packaging plant = likely Class I, NB required only without harmonised standards.[^4]

**The EU ENISA SME readiness survey (June 2026)** found only 35% of respondents use SBOMs, and incident response capability is the weakest domain  — reinforcing the consulting opportunity around exactly the CRA work program you explored in your earlier question.[^5]
